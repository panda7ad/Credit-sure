THE IDEA

More context.
More thoughtful review.

Credit-Sure is a small research workspace for examining alternative signals that may help describe a borrower’s financial circumstances.

No signup is required. Inputs are processed for the current estimate and are not saved as assessment records. The current model is experimental: its estimates are not verified lending advice, a loan offer, or a decision about eligibility.

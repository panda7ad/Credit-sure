THE RESEARCH APPROACH

How it works

Credit-Sure explores whether affordability and repayment behaviour can add useful context where a traditional credit record is limited.

01

Enter a small set of details

The assessment form asks for core application information. Additional behavioural details are optional. If you leave a model input blank, the research model fills it with a value learned from its training data; that can make the estimate less informative.

02

Review an experimental estimate

The current model was developed using public Home Credit competition data and derived feature proxies. It has not been calibrated or validated for live Indian lending. A result is a research output, not a credit score issued by a bureau, a recommendation to lend, or a decision about a real application.

03

Your records stay with your account

Assessments you save are associated with the signed-in Credit-Sure account that created them. Database row-level access rules restrict each account to its own records. Do not enter real customer data until you have a lawful basis, informed consent, an approved privacy notice and production security review.

Important

This prototype is for portfolio and research use only. It is not designed or approved for use in real lending, eligibility decisions, pricing, collections or other consequential decisions about individuals.
